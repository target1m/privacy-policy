Privacy Policy for Batak Offline

Batak Offline is a single-player implementation of Batak, a traditional Turkish trick-taking card game, for mobile devices. The Game works fully offline. This Privacy Policy explains the limited data the App stores on your device and confirms that the App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

1. Introduction

Batak Offline ("we," "our," "the App," or "the Game") is a casual single-player card game in which you play against computer opponents. We respect your privacy and have designed the Game to handle as little data as possible. The Game works entirely offline: it does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, does not contain advertisements, and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. The only data the App creates is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

2. No Internet Connection & No Data Collection

The App is designed to function 100% offline. It makes no network requests and sends no data to us or to any third party. Because nothing ever leaves your device, we have no servers, no database, and no access to any information about you or how you play.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device (in iOS / Android local app storage) and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Player Name: An optional nickname you may type in Settings to show on your seat. It stays on your device.
  • Avatar Selection: The character portrait you choose for yourself.
  • Table Theme: The visual table skin you have selected (Emerald, Ruby, or Sapphire).
  • Sound Settings: Your sound-effect volume / on-off preference.
  • Game Progress & Scores: The state of your current match and standings, so the Game behaves correctly between sessions.

You can permanently erase all of this data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, login, or registration of any kind
  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address is ever sent to us (any nickname you enter stays on your device)
  • No location data (no GPS, no Wi-Fi positioning)
  • No photos, videos, audio recordings, microphone, or camera access
  • No contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • No device identifiers, advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID), or analytics
  • No social-network profile information
  • No payment or billing information (the Game has no in-app purchases)
  • No cookies or other tracking technologies

5. How Information Is Used

The locally stored data described above is used only to run the Game on your device — to remember your nickname, avatar, table theme, and sound preference, and to keep your current match and standings consistent between sessions. It is never used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose, and it never leaves your device. We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect or receive any.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect or transmit data — no analytics services, no advertising networks, no social networks, and no remote crash-reporting services. As a result, no information about you is shared with, or made available to, any third party.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Because the App neither collects nor stores any personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to disclose, even in response to a legal request.

8. Data Retention

  • Local data on your device: Kept until you uninstall the App or clear its app data.
  • Our servers: We retain nothing, because we operate no servers and receive no data from the App.

9. Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's operating-system sandboxing and by any device-level security you have enabled (passcode, biometric lock, full-disk encryption). No data is transmitted over the internet, which removes an entire category of transmission-related risk. No method of local storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, KVKK)

Privacy laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK — Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu) grant you rights to access, correct, delete, port, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Because the App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our side, we hold no personal information about you to access, export, or delete. You remain in full control at all times: every piece of locally stored data can be removed instantly by uninstalling the App. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not process any data that would require consent.

11. Children's Privacy

Batak Offline is a family-friendly card game. It is not directed primarily at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because the App collects no personal data at all. The Game contains no chat, no social features, no advertisements, and no links to external sites.

Where applicable, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. If you have any concern about children's privacy, please contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes to the App or to legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page. Your continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Your Consent

By downloading and using Batak Offline, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and consent to the limited, on-device data handling described above. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the Game.
