Privacy Policy for Forbidden Words

Forbidden Words is a party word game for families and friends on iOS and Android: one player describes a secret word without saying the forbidden words on the card while their team guesses. The game works offline, needs no account, shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. The only information that leaves your device is anonymous usage statistics collected through Google Analytics for Firebase, described in section 3.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Forbidden Words ("we," "our," "the App," or "the Game"), developed by Target1m, handles information when you use the App on iOS or Android. The App is published under localized names in some languages; this policy covers every version of it. We have designed the Game to handle as little data as possible.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device (in iOS / Android local app storage) and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Language: The language you chose for the App.
  • Recent games: Team names, scores and dates of your recent games, shown on the scoreboard. Team names are typed by the players and stay on the device.
  • Recently played cards: Which word cards came up in recent games, so the next games show fresh cards.
  • Settings: Game settings and whether the "How to play" guide has already been shown.

You can erase all of this data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings.

3. Anonymous Usage Statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the App is used and to keep it working well, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. It automatically collects:

  • App activity: App opens, screens viewed and session information (for example, how often and how long the App is used).
  • Device and app information: Device model, operating system version, App version and device language.
  • An app-instance identifier: A random identifier created for this installation of the App. It is not your name, email address or advertising identifier, and it is reset when you reinstall the App.
  • Approximate location: Country or region, which Google derives from the device's IP address. We do not receive or store IP addresses.
We use these statistics only to count how many people use the App and on which devices, to find and fix problems, and to decide which languages and features to improve. They are not used for advertising, are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. Collection of the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA) and ad personalization are turned off in the App.

Google processes this information on our behalf as a service provider. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and kept for no longer than 14 months. You can read how Google handles data at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, login, or registration of any kind
  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • No precise location (no GPS)
  • No photos, videos, audio recordings, microphone, or camera access
  • No contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID)
  • No payment or billing information (the App has no in-app purchases)
  • No chat, messaging, or social features

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App contains no advertisements and no social-network or advertising SDKs. The only third-party service it uses is Google Analytics for Firebase, as described in section 3.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Usage statistics are processed by Google only as our service provider. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Forbidden Words is a family game designed to be played together by children and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the App has no account, no chat and no advertising, and it never asks players for personal details. The anonymous usage statistics described in section 3 are used only to support the internal operation of the App (understanding and improving how it works); they are never used to contact a child, to build a profile of a child, or for advertising.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR and similar laws where applicable. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time with questions or requests using the details below.

8. Data Retention

  • Local data on your device: Kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Usage statistics: Kept by Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months, then deleted automatically.

9. Data Security

Local data is protected by your device's operating-system sandboxing and any device-level security you have enabled. Usage statistics are sent over encrypted (TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, KVKK)

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) give you rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the App does not ask for your identity, the usage statistics are not linked to you; you can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the App, and you can contact us to ask questions or make a request. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes to the App or to legal requirements. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
