Receptionist

Privacy Policy for Receptionist

Applies to the Receptionist iPhone and Android apps, the web desk at reception.st, and the Receptionist Runner app for Mac and Linux.

Receptionist is an issue tracker for teams that work with Claude Code agents. People file issues (often with a screenshot), agents on the team's own machines pick them up, build them on a branch and hand them in for review, and a person merges. To do that we keep your account, the work your team puts into its workspaces, and the information needed to deliver notifications. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

1. Who we are

Receptionist is operated by T1M LLC ("Target1M", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. For anything about your data or your account, write to admin@target1m.io.

Inside a workspace, the team that owns it decides what goes in: issues, comments, files, projects and the repositories they connect. For that content we act on the team's behalf.

2. What we collect

2.1 Your account

  • Name and email address, and a profile photo if you add one (or a drawn avatar you pick).
  • How you sign in. Sign-in runs through Google Firebase Authentication: with Google or Apple we receive your name, email address and a user identifier from them (with Apple you may share a private relay address); with an email and password, Firebase keeps the password and sends the email that confirms your address and the one that resets your password. We never see or store your password, or your Google or Apple password.
  • Sessions: which devices are signed in, with their browser or app version (user agent), so you can sign out everywhere.

2.2 What you and your team put into workspaces

  • Workspaces, projects, members, roles and invitations.
  • Issues, comments, checklists, labels, due dates and the history of each issue.
  • Photos, screenshots and files you attach. The apps use the camera or photo library only when you choose to attach a picture; we store only the pictures you send.
  • The record of agent work on each issue: branches, commits, pull requests, reviews, test and pipeline runs, and their logs.
  • Optional connections you add: a GitHub access token (to open and merge pull requests for you), project environment values (.env), CI/CD settings, and a Telegram link for notifications. Tokens and environment values are stored encrypted.

2.3 Agents and the Runner

Agents run on your own computers, through the Receptionist Runner app or your own Claude Code setup, under your own Claude account. We receive what they report to the desk: the machine's name, the Runner's version and operating system, which issue each agent is working on, its status, and the work it hands in. The code itself stays in your repositories; Claude Code's use of your code is governed by your agreement with Anthropic, not by us.

2.4 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers your device with Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging and sends us the resulting push token, linked to your signed-in session. We use it only to tell you about your issues (for example a review waiting for you). Signing out, or turning notifications off, removes or stops it.

2.5 Technical information

  • IP address, browser or app version and request times in our server logs, used to keep the service secure and working (for example to limit sign-in attempts).
  • When a new account is created, our operators get a short internal notice with the account's name, email, the country Cloudflare places the request in, and whether it came from the web or a phone app.

2.6 What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no advertising, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No precise location, contacts, calendar, health or financial data, and no in-app purchases.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the apps.

3. How we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, show your workspaces, let agents pick up and hand in work, and let people review and merge it.
  • To send the notifications you choose (push, Telegram) and the account emails (confirm your address, reset your password).
  • To keep the service secure: rate limits, spotting abuse, ending sessions after a password reset.
  • To answer you when you write to us, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Turkey)

  • Contract: providing the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping it secure and improving it, in ways that don't override your rights.
  • Consent: push notifications, camera and photo access, and optional connections, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation, where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and workspaces: for as long as the account exists.
  • Sign-in sessions expire 60 days after you sign in, or when you sign out; push tokens go with their session.
  • Server logs: kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, without contacting us:

  • In the app (iPhone or Android): Settings → Delete account.
  • On the web: sign in at reception.st → Settings → Profile → Delete account.
  • By email: write to admin@target1m.io from the address of your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your name, email, photo and sign-in (including the record Firebase keeps for it), your sessions and devices, your agents and Runners, your GitHub tokens and Telegram link, and every workspace only you are in, with all its projects, issues and files. In workspaces shared with others, the issues, comments and history you wrote stay for your team, shown as "Deleted account" instead of your name. If you are the only owner of a workspace that others are in, make someone else an owner first.

8. Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Access and refresh tokens are short-lived and rotated, reused refresh tokens end the session, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and tokens and environment values are encrypted at rest. Passwords are held by Firebase Authentication, never by us. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, please tell us at admin@target1m.io.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, KVKK and similar laws) you can ask us to:

  • access your personal data or receive a copy in a portable format;
  • correct it (you can change your name and photo in Settings yourself);
  • delete it (see section 7);
  • restrict or object to some processing, and withdraw consent;
  • and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Write to admin@target1m.io. We answer within 30 days. We never sell or "share" personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't treat you differently for using your rights.

10. International transfers

Our servers are in the European Union (Frankfurt). Some providers (such as Google, Apple and Cloudflare) may process data in other countries, including the United States; where data leaves the EEA or UK, they rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

11. Children

Receptionist is a work tool for software teams and is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.
