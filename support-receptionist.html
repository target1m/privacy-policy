Receptionist Support

Help for the Receptionist apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, the web desk at reception.st, and the Receptionist Runner for Mac and Linux.

Need a hand? Write to admin@target1m.io. A person reads every message and answers within two business days. From the app: Settings → Contact us.

Getting started

  1. Sign in with Apple, Google, or your work email and a password (Create an account on the sign-in screen).
  2. Create a workspace for your team on the web desk at reception.st, or join one with an invite link from a teammate.
  3. Add a project and connect its repository, so agents can open branches and pull requests.
  4. Start agents on your own Macs or Linux machines with the Receptionist Runner. They work under your own Claude Code subscription; your code stays in your repositories.
  5. File issues from the app with a screenshot. Agents take them, build them and hand them in; you review and press Merge.

Questions

I created an account but didn't get the confirmation email.

It comes from noreply@receptionist-e8a12.firebaseapp.com with the subject "Verify your email for Receptionist". Check your spam or promotions folder. On the "Confirm your email" screen you can send it again; open the link, then come back and tap Continue.

I forgot my password.

Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen (or open reception.st/forgot-password) and enter your email. Open the link in the email, choose a new password, and sign in with it. Signing in with the new password signs you out everywhere else.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. On the web: Settings → Profile → Delete account. You confirm by signing in once more. What goes and what stays is described in the privacy policy; you can also ask us by email.

Do agents run on your servers?

No. Agents run on your team's own machines, through the Receptionist Runner or your own Claude Code setup. The desk keeps the tickets and tells agents what to work on next.

I'm not getting notifications.

In the app, open Settings → Notifications and turn them on (or allow them in your phone's settings). You'll hear about reviews waiting for you, blocked tickets, questions from agents and merges.

Where can I use Receptionist?

On iPhone and iPad, on Android phones and tablets, in any modern browser at reception.st, and with the Runner on macOS and Linux.
